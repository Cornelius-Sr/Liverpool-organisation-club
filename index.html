About the Club

Liverpool Football Club was founded in 1892 and plays its home matches at Anfield. The club competes in the English Premier League and is known worldwide for its passionate supporters, its red kit and its famous anthem, "You'll Never Walk Alone".

Our History

Key moments in the story of the club

Timeline

1892: The Club Is Founded

Liverpool FC was formed in March 1892 after a dispute over the rent of Anfield. John Houlding founded the new club to play at the ground.

1959: Bill Shankly Arrives

Manager Bill Shankly transformed the club, building the foundation of its modern identity and winning league titles and the FA Cup.

1977: First European Cup

Liverpool won its first European Cup, followed by further European successes in 1978, 1981 and 1984 under managers Bob Paisley and Joe Fagan.

2005: The Istanbul Final

Liverpool came back from three goals down in the Champions League final to win the competition for the fifth time.

2019: Sixth European Title

The club won the Champions League for a sixth time, beating Tottenham Hotspur in the final in Madrid.

2020 and 2025: League Champions

Liverpool ended a 30-year wait for the league title in 2020 and won the Premier League again in 2025 to reach 20 league titles.

Mohamed Salah

Forward · Egypt · Liverpool 2017–2026

Mohamed Salah joined Liverpool from Roma in 2017 and became one of the greatest forwards in the club's history. The club's own record shows 255 goals and 119 assists in 435 appearances, making him the third-highest goalscorer in Liverpool's history behind Ian Rush and Roger Hunt.

  • Won the Premier League twice (2019–20 and 2024–25) and the Champions League in 2019.
  • Also won the FA Cup, two League Cups, the FIFA Club World Cup and the UEFA Super Cup.
  • Premier League Golden Boot winner four times (2017–18, 2018–19, 2021–22, 2024–25).
  • Three-time PFA Players' Player of the Year and winner of the FIFA Puskas Award in 2018.

"You'll Never Walk Alone"

Why Liverpool fans sing the anthem

Where the Song Comes From

"You'll Never Walk Alone" was written by Richard Rodgers and Oscar Hammerstein II for the 1945 musical Carousel. In 1963, the Liverpool band Gerry and the Pacemakers recorded their own version, and it reached number one in the UK charts.

How It Became Liverpool's Anthem

In the early 1960s, the songs in the top ten were played over the speakers at Anfield before matches, and the fans on the Kop sang along. When the song dropped out of the charts, the supporters kept singing it, and it became part of match day. Manager Bill Shankly also embraced it, and the words are now written on the Shankly Gates outside the ground.

Why Fans Sing It

A message of hope

The song is about carrying on through hard times with hope. Fans sing it to show that the team will never be left to face a challenge alone.

Unity

Thousands of supporters sing together before kick-off, which reminds everyone in the ground that the club is a community, not just a team.

Remembrance

After the Hillsborough disaster in 1989, in which 97 Liverpool supporters died, the song took on a deeper meaning of solidarity and remembrance for the club and the city.

Tradition

It is sung before every home game, handed down from one generation of fans to the next.
